Adaptive Memory
for Research Agents

Lin et al. · 2026

3. Method

The agent stores a compact record of verified evidence and retrieves only the entries relevant to the current research question.

Each record retains the source document and page location. Before a note is revised, the current content is checked against the saved snapshot.

3.1 Evidence and attribution

A claim is linked to the passage that supports it. Missing or unreadable evidence is reported explicitly.
